# User Guide (user-guide.md)

Welcome to Najmah! This guide walks you through creating and reading custom, illustrated stories.

---

## 1. Getting Started
1. Access the platform landing page and register a parent account.
2. Complete verification and log in.
3. Access the **Family** section and create a child profile, specifying their age, language, and interests.

---

## 2. Creating a Story
1. Click **Create Story** on the Parent Dashboard.
2. Select the target child profile, language, and story theme (e.g. "Space Adventure").
3. Select an SEL Goal (e.g. "Overcoming Fear").
4. Click **Generate Story**. The platform will plan, write, and validate the story.
5. Once complete, the story appears in the child's story library.

---

## 3. Reading and Audio Narrations
1. Open any story in the **Library**.
2. Customize font sizes using the reader configuration panel.
3. Toggle the **Audio Narrator** to play high-quality, synthetic voice readings of the story pages.
4. Click the **Illustrate** button on any page to render custom, scene-appropriate illustrations.
